# Governance Fix

> A narrow and technocratic approach to governing generative AI. (Ulnicane 2025)

## Characteristics

1. **Risk management framing**: Focus on mitigating risks rather than directing innovation purpose
2. **Narrow participation**: Limited to developed countries, experts, industry
3. **Technocratic**: Depoliticized, treats governance as technical problem
4. **Neglects direction**: Ignores questions of what AI should be developed and for whom

## The Paradox

Generative AI is widely USED by the public, but its governance is narrow and elite-dominated. This is the "paradox of generative AI governance."

## Alternative: Politics of Polycentric Governance

Instead of governance fix, embrace:
- Democratic co-shaping of technology
- Wide-ranging public participation
- Government role in reshaping power imbalances (concentrated in big tech)
- Responsible Innovation approach
