# Governance Fix: Power and Politics in Generative AI

**Ulnicane (2025)**

## Core Contribution

Analyzes the first international governance initiatives for generative AI (G7 Hiroshima, OECD reports, UK AI Safety Summit). **Coins the term "governance fix"** — a narrow, technocratic approach that frames governance as risk management while neglecting purpose, direction, and public participation.

## Key Concepts

- **Governance fix**: Narrow risk-management framing; neglects innovation purpose/direction; limited public roles
- **Paradox of generative AI governance**: Widely used by public, but governance is narrow (developed countries, experts, industry)
- **Polycentric but unequal**: Multiple centers of decision-making but dominated by few developed countries
- **Alternative**: Politics of polycentric governance + Responsible Innovation
