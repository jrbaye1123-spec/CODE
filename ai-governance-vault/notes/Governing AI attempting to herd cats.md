# Governing AI – Attempting to Herd Cats?

**Büthe, Djeffal, Lütge, Maasen & von Ingersleben-Seip (2022)**

## Core Contribution

Introduction to the JEPP special issue on Governance of AI. The "herding cats" metaphor captures the fundamental challenge: AI governance is inherently polycentric, with multiple independent actors pursuing different goals.

## Key Themes

- **Polycentricity**: AI governed by states, IOs, companies, standards bodies — not hierarchical
- **Fragmentation**: Different regimes, jurisdictions, and standards compete and overlap
- **Coordination challenge**: How to align without central authority?
- **European focus**: EU AI Act, European approach to trustworthy AI

## Bridge to Ulnicane

- Ulnicane's work fills the gap Büthe identifies: governance is not just regulation — it's politics, framing, power
- "Governance fix" (Ulnicane 2025) is what happens when herding cats narrows to a few dominant actors
- Both emphasize the need for broader public participation
