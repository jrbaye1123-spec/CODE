# Ethics Washing

> When powerful actors focus on ethics guidelines as a way to avoid or delay binding regulation. (Ulnicane et al. 2021)

## The Pattern

1. AI company/actor faces public concern about their technology
2. They announce ethics principles, guidelines, or an ethics board
3. These are non-binding, often vague, and lack enforcement mechanisms
4. Public concern is mollified without meaningful change in behavior
5. Binding regulation is delayed or avoided

## Why It Works

- Ethics guidelines appear responsible
- They require no legal compliance
- They can be selectively interpreted
- They shift burden from regulator to regulated
- Multi-stakeholder forums can be captured by well-resourced actors

## The Alternative: Good Governance

Ulnicane et al. (2021) propose going beyond ethics + regulation:
- Cooperation/coordination between state and non-state actors
- International research collaboration
- Responsible Innovation approach
- Democratic participation in technology shaping
