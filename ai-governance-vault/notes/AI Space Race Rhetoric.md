# AI Space Race Rhetoric

> Framing AI development as a rivalry among superpowers where one country will win and others will be left behind.

## Three Problematic Aspects (Ulnicane 2023)

1. **Zero-sum framing**: Technology development globally as win-lose
2. **Deregulatory pressure**: Governments told to regulate less to "not fall behind"
3. **High-tech over social**: Prioritizes technologies over social issues

## The Critique

"Space dreams are problematic if the result is that earthly problems are neglected" (Coeckelbergh 2020)

- AI space race rhetoric serves narrow short-term economic interests
- Neglects broader longer-term societal needs
- Ignores possibilities for international cooperation
- Distorts policy priorities toward competition over welfare

## Alternative: Cooperation + Competition

Ulnicane proposes moving beyond the binary to recognize:
- AI development involves both competition AND cooperation
- International research collaboration is essential
- Global challenges (climate, health) need AI cooperation, not AI warfare
- The public should shape what AI is developed, not just accept competition-driven outcomes
