# Against the New Space Race

**Ulnicane (2023)**

## Core Argument

Critically interrogates the use of "space race" rhetoric in AI discussions. Three problems:
1. Perceiving tech development globally as zero-sum game
2. Pressuring governments to regulate less
3. Prioritizing high technologies over social issues

## Key Quote

> "Space dreams are problematic if the result is that earthly problems are neglected" (Coeckelbergh 2020)

## Alternative

Open up conversations about alternative futures — AI for people, not superpower rivalry. Cooperation alongside competition.
