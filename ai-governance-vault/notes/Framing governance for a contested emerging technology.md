# Framing Governance for a Contested Emerging Technology

**Ulnicane et al. (2020)**

## Core Contribution

Examines how AI policy documents **frame governance** as a way to resolve public controversies about AI benefits and concerns. Part of the Policy & Society special issue on AI and robotics governance.

## Key Findings

1. **Policy learning**: Countries learn from each other and from WEF, OECD
2. **Benefits framing**: AI benefits extend beyond economic growth to Grand Societal Challenges
3. **Government roles**: Diverse — promoting AI, mitigating risks, encouraging stakeholder involvement
4. **Multi-stakeholder challenges**: Hard to ensure balanced representation in practice

## Bridge to CMI (Meta)

This paper is the *methodological* heart of Ulnicane's programme: analyzing HOW governance is framed reveals WHO has power and WHAT interests are served. Applied across all her other papers.
