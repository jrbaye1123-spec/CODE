# Responsible Innovation (RRI)

Democratic and participatory co-shaping of technology for social benefit.

## Core Dimensions (Stilgoe et al.)

- **Anticipation**: Foreseeing possible impacts (not prediction, but preparation)
- **Reflexivity**: Examining one's own assumptions, values, and framings
- **Inclusion**: Bringing diverse publics and stakeholders into the conversation
- **Responsiveness**: Actually changing course based on what is learned

## In AI Governance

RRI is positioned as an alternative to:
1. **Ethics washing** — superficial principles without accountability
2. **Governance fix** — narrow technocratic risk management
3. **AI space race** — zero-sum competition neglecting social issues

## Key Tension

```
Innovation speed (industry: "don't slow us down")
        vs.
Responsible deliberation (society: "who benefits and at what cost?")
```

RRI argues these are not opposed: responsible innovation is better innovation.
