# Governance of Responsible AI

**Gianni, Lehtinen & Nieminen (2022)**

## Core Contribution

Argues for moving from ethical guidelines to **cooperative policies** for responsible AI. Ethics guidelines alone are insufficient; governance structures must enable cooperative action.

## Key Argument

1. Ethics guidelines proliferate but lack enforcement
2. Regulation is necessary but insufficient alone
3. **Cooperative policies** bridge the gap: multi-stakeholder, deliberative, adaptable
4. Responsible AI requires institutional infrastructure, not just principles

## Bridge to Ulnicane

- Directly supports Ulnicane's "beyond ethics guidelines" argument
- Cooperative policies ≈ Ulnicane's "good governance" alternative
- Both identify the ethics-to-governance gap
- Contrast with ethics washing: genuine cooperative vs. performative ethical posture
