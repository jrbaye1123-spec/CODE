# A Layered Model for AI Governance

**Gasser & Almeida (2017)**

## Core Contribution

A three-layer conceptual framework for AI governance to bridge the information asymmetry between developers and policymakers:

1. **Social and Technical Layer**: What AI systems do, their capabilities and limitations
2. **Institutional Layer**: Laws, regulations, standards, norms
3. **Individual Layer**: Impacts on individuals, their rights and remedies

## Key Insight

AI-based systems are "black boxes" — massive information asymmetries between developers and consumers/policymakers. The layered model helps structure governance interventions at the right level.
