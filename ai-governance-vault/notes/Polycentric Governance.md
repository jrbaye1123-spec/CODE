# Polycentric Governance

Multiple overlapping centers of decision-making in collaborative (not purely competitive) relationships.

## Origin
From Vincent and Elinor Ostrom's work on common-pool resource governance. Challenged the dichotomy of state vs. market solutions, showing communities can self-govern.

## In AI Governance

AI governance is inherently polycentric:
- **States**: National AI strategies, regulations (EU AI Act, US Executive Orders)
- **International Organizations**: OECD AI Principles, UNESCO AI Ethics
- **Industry**: Self-regulation, standards bodies (ISO, IEEE)
- **Civil Society**: Advocacy, watchdog, participatory forums
- **Multi-stakeholder**: G7 Hiroshima Process, UK AI Safety Summit

## The Paradox of Generative AI Governance (Ulnicane 2025)

> While generative AI is widely used by the public, its governance is rather narrow.

Polycentric governance in generative AI is dominated by a limited number of developed countries. The public uses the technology but is largely excluded from its governance.
