# Politics of Public Research Funding

**Ulnicane (2023)**

## Core Argument

EU Framework Programme expanded through three policy frames:
1. **Economic competitiveness** (initial driver)
2. **Scientific excellence** (further expansion)
3. **Societal challenges** (recent expansion)

Highlights that research funding is NOT technocratic but deeply political — values, interests, and priorities shape who gets funded and for what.
