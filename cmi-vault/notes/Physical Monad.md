# Physical Monad

> "A scaffold is not just a material. It is a physical monad: an autonomous, history-dependent differential machine whose state is a section of a fiber bundle over its own configuration space."

## Leibnizian Origin

Leibniz's monads are:
- **Windowless** — their internal principle fully determines their evolution
- **Self-contained** — each monad mirrors the universe from its own perspective
- **Driven by internal principle** — no external forces needed to explain change

## CMI's Physical Monad

The scaffold-as-monad:
- **Autonomous**: The degradation is governed by ∇self, which the scaffold defines from its own state
- **History-dependent**: Fg accumulates irreversible memory; two scaffolds with identical *current* state but different histories diverge
- **Self-referential**: The operator that governs evolution is part of what is being evolved

## Why "Physical" Monad

Unlike Leibniz's metaphysical monads:
- The physical monad is **falsifiable** (see [[CMI-Falsification]])
- It is **materially instantiated** in GO/PLGA-PEG/HA
- Its "internal principle" is ∇self — a measurable geometric object
