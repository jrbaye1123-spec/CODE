# On the Failure of Anisotropic Materials

**Bridge to CMI**: Anisotropic failure criteria provide the limiting cases of CMI's degradation dynamics. When ∇self curvature exceeds a critical threshold (analogous to a failure surface in stress space), the scaffold transitions from controlled degradation to catastrophic failure. The anisotropy of GO sheets (planar orientation) makes the failure criterion direction-dependent — a geometric effect.
