# G-structures and Material Homogeneity

**Bridge to CMI**: G-structures are the mathematical formalism for material symmetry groups. A material with a given symmetry group (e.g., isotropy = O(3), transverse isotropy) defines a G-structure on its body manifold. CMI's covariant atlas treats different material descriptions as coordinate charts connected by G-structure transition functions. The groupoid approach in [[Unified geometric formulation of material uniformity and evolution]] generalizes G-structures.
