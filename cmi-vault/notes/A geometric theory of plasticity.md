# A Geometric Theory of Plasticity

**Panoskaltsis, Soldatos & Triantafyllou (2011)**

## Core Contribution

A geometric formulation of rate-independent generalized plasticity using the **physical (referential) metric as a primary internal variable**. Crucially, does NOT invoke any decomposition of kinematical quantities into elastic and plastic parts.

## Key Results

1. **Metric as primary internal variable**: The referential metric g replaces the plastic strain tensor as the fundamental evolving quantity
2. **No multiplicative decomposition**: Avoids the Lee decomposition (F = Fe·Fp) entirely
3. **Covariant balance of energy**: Systematically employed to derive mechanical state equations
4. **Doyle-Ericksen failure**: Shown that in finite plasticity, covariant energy balance does NOT yield Doyle-Ericksen formula without further assumptions
5. **Lie derivative framework**: Evolution of metric governed by Lie derivative along flow of plastic evolution

## Bridge to CMI

- Panoskaltsis's **physical metric** is a precursor to CMI's **state-dependent metric** on the configuration manifold
- The "metric as evolving internal variable" is exactly the mechanism CMI uses for ∇self
- The failure of Doyle-Ericksen in plasticity mirrors CMI's claim that standard thermodynamics fails for self-referential systems
- Both frameworks reject decomposition into elastic/plastic parts in favor of unified geometric evolution
