# A Constructive Approach of Invariants

**Bridge to CMI**: Constructive methods for building invariants from material tensors. CMI's Noether charge Q = Tr(F∧F) and the degradation rate α are both invariants — quantities that don't depend on the coordinate description. This paper provides the systematic approach for constructing such invariants from the material state, which is exactly what CMI needs to identify measurable predictions (α, Q, ηγ₀) from the geometric structure.
