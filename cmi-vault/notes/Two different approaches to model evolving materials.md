# Two Different Approaches to Model Evolving Materials

**Bridge to CMI**: Explicit comparison of approaches to modeling materials that change over time. CMI's contribution is a third approach: the self-referential approach where the evolution operator itself evolves. This paper's two approaches likely represent the traditional paradigms (fixed-operator and externally-updated-operator) that CMI transcends.
