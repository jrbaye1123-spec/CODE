# Guidelines for Constructing Strain Gradient Theories

**Bridge to CMI**: Strain gradient theories incorporate higher-order spatial derivatives (∇²u, ∇³u...) to capture size effects. CMI's ∇self is effectively an infinite-order gradient theory: it depends on the full history-dependent state, not just local gradients. The guidelines for constructing well-posed strain gradient theories inform how to construct well-posed ∇self operators.
