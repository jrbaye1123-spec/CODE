# The Incompatibility Operator from Riemannian Geometry

**Bridge to CMI**: The incompatibility operator (curl of the curl of strain) measures when a strain field cannot be derived from a displacement field — i.e., when defects are present. This is the geometric origin of Fg in CMI: plastic strain Fg is precisely the incompatibility that prevents the configuration from being embedded in flat Euclidean space. The curvature of ∇self measures this incompatibility.
