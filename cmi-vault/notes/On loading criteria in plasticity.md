# On Loading Criteria in Plasticity

**Bridge to CMI**: Loading criteria determine when plastic (irreversible) deformation begins. CMI's degradation front is an irreversible front — it advances when configurational "loading" (curvature of ∇self) exceeds a threshold. The loading criteria of classical plasticity generalize to CMI's criteria for when the scaffold transitions from reversible swelling to irreversible hydrolysis.
