# Noether Charge in Materials

> **Q = Tr(F ∧ F)** — the topological invariant measuring GO sheet winding.

## Origin

From Noether's theorem: every continuous symmetry of a Lagrangian yields a conserved current. In CMI, the gauge symmetry of the GO sheets yields the conserved charge Q.

## Physical Meaning

- **F** is the curvature 2-form of the gauge connection associated with GO sheets
- **Tr(F ∧ F)** is the Pontryagin class — a topological invariant
- Q measures the **total winding** of GO sheets in the scaffold
- Conservation means: GO sheets cannot be created or destroyed, only rearranged

## Falsification Role

If Q drifts during degradation:
- The gauge structure is falsified
- GO sheets are not actually a gauge field
- Something deeper is needed

## Connection to Configurational Forces

The [[Configurational forces as dissipative mechanisms]] paper establishes that configurational work equals net dissipation iff Euler-Lagrange equations hold. CMI's Noether charge is the *conserved* counterpart to those *dissipative* configurational forces.
