## The Deeper Pattern

Every one of these 31 papers describes a system where:
1. A **geometric quantity** (metric, connection, curvature, incompatibility, groupoid)
2. **Evolves** with the material state
3. And this evolution **depends on** the geometric quantity itself

CMI names this pattern **∇self** and makes it the central equation: **Φ = ∇self · d**.
