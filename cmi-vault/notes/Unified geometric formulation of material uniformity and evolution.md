# Unified Geometric Formulation of Material Uniformity and Evolution

**Epstein & de León (2016)** — Dedicated to Gérard Maugin

## Core Contribution

A differential-geometric framework using **groupoids subordinate to geometric distributions** to unify:
- Standard theory of material uniformity
- Extended theory of functionally graded materials
- Temporal counterparts in anelastic and aging processes

## Key Concepts

- **Groupoids** generalize groups: symmetries between different points, not just symmetries at a point
- **Geometric distributions**: smooth assignments of subspaces of the tangent bundle
- **Material uniformity**: two points are materially isomorphic if connected by a material symmetry
- **Evolution**: time-dependent changes in the material groupoid
