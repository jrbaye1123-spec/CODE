# Rosen Closure Condition

> η_HA γ₀ = 1

## What It Means

From Robert Rosen's relational biology:
- **Metabolism** (M-map): the material processes that maintain the system
- **Repair** (R-map): the processes that repair the metabolic machinery
- **Closure**: R(M) = M — repair maps onto metabolism, forming a closed loop

In CMI:
- η_HA = repair efficiency (function of HA concentration)
- γ₀ = baseline metabolic rate
- η_HA γ₀ = 1 ⇒ repair exactly compensates metabolism

## Why It Matters for Scaffolds

Hydroxyapatite (HA) provides the "repair" function:
- Buffers pH changes from PLGA hydrolysis
- Provides calcium ions for mineralization
- Maintains scaffold integrity

The Rosen closure condition says there is a **critical HA concentration** where repair and degradation balance. Below this threshold, the scaffold cannot sustain itself.

## Falsification

If η_HA γ₀ ≠ 1 at any HA concentration, then the scaffold is not a Rosen (M,R)-system — it is merely a passive substrate.
