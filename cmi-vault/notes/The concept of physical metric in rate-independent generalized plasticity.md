# The Concept of Physical Metric in Rate-Independent Generalized Plasticity

**Panoskaltsis, Soldatos & Triantafyllou (2011)**

## Core Contribution

Extended treatment of the **physical (referential) metric as primary internal variable** in generalized plasticity. Uses invariance under flow of a vector field as the geometric argument for transitioning to classical plasticity.

## Key Points

- Rejects decomposition of kinematical quantities into elastic and plastic parts
- Cites Gilman's critique: "It seems very unfortunate that the theory of plasticity was ever cast into the mold of stress-strain relations"
- Uses **invariance of sets under Lie flow** as the geometric criterion for plasticity onset
- Covariant energy balance employed systematically

## Bridge to CMI

- The rejection of elastic/plastic decomposition mirrors CMI's unified ∇self approach
- Physical metric evolution is a concrete precursor to CMI's state-manifold metric evolution
- The Lie derivative flow criterion connects to CMI's path-dependence (different histories → different ∇self)
