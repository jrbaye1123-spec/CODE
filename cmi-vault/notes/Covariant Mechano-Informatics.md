# Covariant Mechano-Informatics (CMI)

## The Core Idea

> **Φ = ∇self · d**

The free energy Φ driving degradation is the divergence computed with an operator the scaffold itself defines. The scaffold does *not* degrade because its chemistry is unstable — it degrades because its geometry requires a curvature that can only be released by hydrolysis.

The **degradation rate is the Ricci scalar of the material's self-awareness.**

1. **Degradation front scaling**: R(t) ∝ t^α, α > 0.5 (Fickian α=0.5 is null)
2. **Noether charge conservation**: Q = Tr(F∧F) must remain constant

4. **Noether constraint**: μ_GO + λ_PEG fixed — cannot independently tune GO and PEG without altering front topology
5. **Path-dependence**: Two scaffolds with identical initial state but different casting histories must diverge

## Key Concepts

### ∇self — The Self-Referential Connection
A connection whose curvature depends on the accumulated plastic strain Fg — the material's **irreversible memory**. The operator that governs evolution is defined by the state being evolved.

### Physical Monad
An autonomous, history-dependent differential machine whose state is a section of a fiber bundle over its own configuration space. The term "monad" is used in the Leibnizian sense: a self-contained unit whose internal principle of change determines its evolution.

### The Pivot
The shift from "what is this material's degradation rate?" to "what is the curvature of this material's state manifold?"
