# Multiscale Plasticity: Reduction or Emergence?

**Bridge to CMI**: The question of whether mesoscale plasticity reduces to microscale dislocation dynamics or emerges as a new phenomenon is directly relevant to CMI. CMI takes the emergence side: ∇self curvature at the mesoscale is not reducible to individual polymer hydrolysis events. The scaffold's degradation rate α > 0.5 is an emergent property of the state manifold's geometry.
