# 4-D Geometrical Modeling of Material Aging

**Bridge to CMI**: Treats time as a fourth geometric dimension for modeling material aging. CMI's path-dependence (scaffolds with same state but different histories diverge) is precisely a 4-D geometric effect: the worldline of the scaffold in state-space times time determines ∇self.
