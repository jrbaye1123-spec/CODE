# Gauge Theory of Defects in Elastic Continua

**Sahoo & Valsakumar (2005)**

## Core Contribution

A selective review of gauge theory approaches to defects in elastic continua. Introduces dislocation and disclination density tensors, then surveys both **Yang-Mills type** and **gravity type** gauge theories for defect dynamics.

## Key Concepts

- **Dislocation density tensor** α_ij: measures closure failure of Burgers circuit
- **Disclination density tensor** Θ_ij: measures closure failure of Frank circuit  
- **Yang-Mills approach**: Defects as gauge fields with internal symmetry groups (e.g., SO(3) for rotational defects)
- **Gravity-type approach**: Defects as curvature and torsion of the material manifold (Einstein-Cartan geometry)

## Bridge to CMI

- CMI's **GO gauge field** is a direct descendant of Yang-Mills defect gauge theories
- The **Noether charge Q = Tr(F∧F)** measuring GO winding is the Yang-Mills Pontryagin invariant applied to material gauge fields
- CMI's **∇self** with curvature from Fg generalizes the gravity-type approach: defects = curvature of the material connection
- Sahoo & Valsakumar's review covers the mathematical machinery CMI deploys for GO sheets
