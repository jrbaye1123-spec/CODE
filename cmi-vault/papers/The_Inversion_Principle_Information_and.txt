Abstract
Between May and June 2026, three independent research programs attempting to map information-theoretic observables onto classical geometric
containers reached their regimes of breakdown: holographic neural networks
failed to map attention entropy to Ryu-Takayanagi surfaces; Wheeler-DeWitt
quantum cosmology failed to derive the Riemann ξ functional equation from
compact spatial geometries; and physical analogies for P vs NP collapsed at
the de Sitter fixed point.
In all three cases, the mathematical tools survived while the geometric
containers failed. This perspective synthesizes the aftermath into a unified
framework: The Inversion Principle. We demonstrate that spacetime geometry (Paper I), arithmetic geometry (Paper II), and combinatorial search
(Paper III) are not fundamental realities, but effective field theories of deeper
information-geometric and spectral structures.
By mapping the “surviving toolkit”—logdet conditioning, prolate spectral
concentration, and Fisher-Rao volume bounds—onto Wei’s subregion entanglement, the Connes adèlic program, and the Overlap Gap Property of random 3-SAT, we establish a cross-domain calibration metric (the Φ framework).
We conclude that the great bottlenecks of modern theoretical science are not
geometric, but information-geometric: the universe, the primes, and computational hardness are all governed by the capacity limits of their underlying
statistical manifolds.

Introduction: Three Breakdowns, One Pattern

Between May and June of 2026, three independent research programs reached their
limits. Each had attempted the same structural maneuver: map an informationtheoretic or spectral observable onto a classical geometric object, and extract physical
or mathematical predictions from the mapping. Each failed. And in each case, the
tools survived while the containers collapsed.

1.1 The Holographic Transformer (May 2026)
The conjecture: attention entropy H(attn) in transformer neural networks realizes
the Ryu-Takayanagi holographic entanglement entropy formula. The holographic
screen—the log-determinant of the activation covariance matrix—was proposed as
the bulk-boundary dictionary, mapping layer-wise information geometry onto an
emergent AdS spacetime.
The test: out-of-distribution screen contraction. When the training distribution
was contracted toward the manifold boundary, the gradient linking information geometry to geometric bulk deteriorated. The holographic dictionary did not survive
the deformation. The tools—entropy tracking, Λ-gate saturation metrics, logdet covariance conditioning, Lawvere stationarity loops—remained intact. The holographic
container collapsed.

1.2 Wheeler-DeWitt → Riemann Hypothesis (May–June 2026)
The conjecture: the Wheeler-DeWitt equation on compact spatial slices in unimodular quantum cosmology reproduces the functional equation ξ(s) = ξ(1 − s) of the
completed Riemann xi-function. Four geometric compactifications were tested: flat
FLRW, closed S 3 , toroidal T 3 , and circular T 1 . All four failed.
The root cause was arithmetic: the Γ-factor π −s/2 Γ(s/2) arises from Poisson
summation over the integer lattice Z1 , and no geometric manifold in 3 + 1 dimensions carries this structure intrinsically. The tools—prolate spheroidal wave function
asymptotics, self-adjoint extension classification, spectral density bounds, variational
parity gap estimates—survived. The geometric container collapsed.

1.3 Physical Analogies for P vs NP (May–June 2026)
The conjecture: the cosmological constant Λ toggles P vs NP as a physical parameter.
When Λ > Λcritical , computational complexity classes separate. When Λ ≤ Λcritical ,
they collapse.
The test: Berry phase computation for the Kantowski-Sachs Wheeler-DeWitt
system. The Berry connection was found to be exactly flat (Fβv = 0) for all values
of Λ with real boundary conditions. The discretization that was supposed to toggle
between P and NP was numerical artifact—grid refinement eliminated all apparent
rational denominators. The tools—logdet conditioning, the Fisher Information Metric, intrinsic gradient measurement, spectral concentration analysis—survived. The
physical container collapsed.

1.4 The Shared Pattern
All three programs failed at the same structural juncture: they mapped informationtheoretic or spectral observables onto geometric objects, and the mapping returned
null. The mapping direction was wrong. The correct direction is the inversion:
Geometry is not the object of the mapping. Geometry is the effective
description. The information-geometric and spectral structures are fundamental.

The Φ Framework: Calibration Without Identity

The master formula that survived the falsifications is:
Φ = ∇self · d

native mathematics of a single system—and d is the distance from the de Sitter fixed
point, the state of maximum symmetry where all cross-domain mappings return zero.
At d = 0 (the fixed point): ∇self = 0, all cross-domain analogies return null. This
is not a failure of the formula. It is the formula’s first and most important prediction:
at the point of maximum symmetry, there is nothing to measure across domains.
At d > 0: structure exists. The surviving tools measure it without claiming that
domain A is identical to domain B.

3.1 Kill Criteria
Φ is a measurement apparatus, not a predictive theory. As a measurement apparatus,
it requires no falsification. But if Φ is claimed to explain phenomena, three conditions
must hold:
1. Invariant absence. A system at d = 0 must have ∇self = 0. If not, Φ fails as
a classifier.
2. Non-stationarity. A perturbation away from d = 0 must not spontaneously
return; the fixed point must attract.
3. Cross-domain consistency. Two domains both at d = 0 must not produce
incompatible Φ values.
These criteria distinguish the ontology (the fixed point exists) from the heuristics
(how we measure distance from it).

Paper II: The Connes Spectral Engine

5.1 The Architecture
The Connes–Consani–Moscovici spectral realization of the zeros of the Riemann zeta
function (arXiv:2511.22755) reduces the Riemann Hypothesis to two technical obstacles: Weil positivity of the quantum Weil operator, and selection of the correct
rank-1 perturbation. The spectral engine resolves the analytic obstacles using rigorous perturbation theory, without proving the arithmetic inputs.

5.2 The Inversion
The WDW → RH falsification demonstrated that continuous geometry cannot replicate arithmetic: the Γ-factor requires an integer lattice that cosmology does not
possess. The Connes adèlic framework provides the correct container—arithmetic
geometry, not differential geometry.
The tools that survived the geometric detour—prolate superexponential decay
bounds, variational parity gap estimates, Davis–Kahan sin Θ perturbation theory—
become the analytic engine of the arithmetic program. The engine guarantees det(QWλ ) →
Ξ(1/2 + iz) if two arithmetic inputs are supplied. It does not prove RH. It reduces
RH to two well-defined problems in analytic number theory and spectral geometry.

5.3 The Φ Connection
The Connes engine is the Φ methodology in its purest form: “here is where we
are (the analytic engine), here is what we need (Input A: Weil positivity, Input B:
rank-1 perturbation), here is what happens when they converge (Ξ(1/2 + iz)).” The
conditional architecture separates what has been accomplished from what remains,
without claiming either has been completed. The engine specifies the interface; any
solution that meets the specification triggers the proof.

The Inversion Principle

The three papers, read together, reveal a single structural principle:
In every domain where classical geometric containers failed, the surviving tools were information-geometric or spectral measurements. The
geometric objects—spacetime, arithmetic varieties, combinatorial search
spaces—are effective descriptions. The information-geometric and spectral manifolds from which they emerge are fundamental.
This is the Inversion Principle. It is not a theorem. It is a methodological
discovery: the mapping direction between information theory and geometry that the
research community has pursued for decades (information → geometry) is inverted.
Geometry → information. The geometric objects are the low-energy limits, the effective field theories, the classical limits of deeper information-theoretic structures.

The Inversion Principle does not multiply across domains. It identifies, within

computational complexity, is the same toolkit.

7.2 The De Sitter Fixed Point as Universal Calibrator
The de Sitter fixed point—the state of maximum symmetry where all gradients
vanish—functions as a calibration source for all three domains:
• In quantum gravity: the point where the WDW singular potential vanishes, all
clock choices are unitarily equivalent, and the problem of time dissolves.
• In analytic number theory: the scale λ → ∞ limit where the prolate concentration eigenvalues approach 1 and the Weil explicit formula becomes an
identity.
• In computational complexity: the maximum-entropy uniform distribution over
Boolean functions, where no circuit has an advantage over random guessing.
At this fixed point, Φ = 0 automatically. The five falsifications that preceded
the trilogy were measurements at d = 0—calibrations establishing the origin. The
three papers are measurements at d > 0—the first positive programs. The phase
diagram of research programs—distance from the fixed point vs. falsifiability—is the
structure that replaces the collapsed analogical bridges.

The tools for probing this structure—logdet conditioning, entanglement entropy,
horizon-scale correlations—are already in hand. What remains is the observational
program: detecting the discrete transition spectra and the minimum black hole mass
predicted by the subregion framework.

not by physicists.

immune to the Natural Proofs barrier for precisely this reason.

The Methodological Legacy

The most important output of the 2026 research program is not any single theorem.
It is the methodological discovery that the tools that survive falsification are the
correct descriptions. The holographic transformer was falsified; logdet conditioning
survived. The WDW → RH program was falsified; prolate spectral asymptotics
survived. The physical analogies for P vs NP were falsified; the Fisher Information
Metric survived.
When a cross-domain analogy collapses, extract the intrinsic measurements from
the debris. They are the coordinate axes of the phase diagram. They do not tell you
what to find. They tell you how to measure. The rest is data.
The Φ framework formalizes this: Φ = ∇self · d. The intrinsic gradient, multiplied
by the distance from the fixed point, is the only quantity that survives cross-domain
comparison. It does not claim identity. It claims measurability. And measurability,
in the end, is what separates science from speculation.
