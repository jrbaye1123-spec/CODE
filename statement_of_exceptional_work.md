## Core Contributions

**1. Conditional spectral gap closures (Connes program)**
Derived conditions under which the spectral gap in Dirac-type operators vanishes. The gap closes only under strict operator constraints corresponding to physical boundary conditions. Otherwise, a residual gap persists — with implications for fundamental limits on spectral resolution in quantum systems.

**2. Spectral approach to P vs NP**
Reformulated computational complexity in spectral terms: decision problems map onto eigenvalue spectra of self-adjoint operators on Hilbert space. The P/NP distinction becomes a spectral gap condition on the verification operator — bounded spectral radius implies collapse, otherwise separation. Connects to quantum geometric tensors and phase transitions in optimization.

**3. Consciousness as incomplete self-modeling**
Formal proof that, under mild constraints, a system cannot fully model its own modeling. Phenomenal states are the irreducible gap between total state space and internal representational capacity. This yields a formal bound on interpretability — a sufficiently self-referential system will always contain structure opaque to its own introspection.

**4. Spectral psychiatry**
Applied the same operator-theoretic tools to psychiatric nosology: mental states as continuous spectra rather than discrete DSM categories. Yields testable predictions and a framework for evaluating behavior that resists categorical classification.

**5. Epistemic accounting framework**
Every claim in the 889-page text is tagged [P], [E], [C], or [I]. The result is a fully auditable research program with explicit confidence levels — directly applicable to alignment, evaluation, and model behavior analysis.
