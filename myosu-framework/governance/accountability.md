# Accountability Statement

**Owner:** John (Nakamichi Shinjin / Jacques Myo / Shinjin R. Baye)
**Role:** Sole accountable author for all vault content and all outputs derived from it.
**Effective:** 2026-08-04
**Review:** Quarterly and on any authorship incident.

## What I Own

- Every claim that enters this vault, regardless of whether it was extracted, synthesized, or generated by an agent.
- Every output — manuscript, paper, public statement, private correspondence — that draws on vault content.
- Every error in provenance, attribution, synthesis, or judgment that originates from vault content I approved.
- The decision to publish, to withhold, to correct, or to retract.

## What I Do Not Delegate

- The authority to decide what counts as true within my research domain.
- The authority to collapse multiple interpretive frames into a single thesis.
- The responsibility to verify attribution before publication.
- The duty to correct errors I discover or that are brought to my attention.

## What the Designer Owns

- The technical integrity of transparency mechanisms, review surfaces, and approval workflows.
- The enforcement of provenance metadata persistence through all handoffs.
- The detection and reporting of agent drift, compositional risks, and review-surface inadequacy.
- The fix when a system flaw — not a judgment error — causes an incident.

## What We Share

- Responsibility for recurring failure patterns.
- The weekly governance cadence: review metrics, surface signals, evolve the system.
- The commitment that accountability is not theatrical — it is structural, verifiable, and practiced.

## Breach

If I approve claims I cannot meaningfully review because the review surface is inadequate, the designer has failed me — and I have failed myself by not raising the signal. Both are accountable.

## Signature

This statement is human-authored. No agent synthesis.
