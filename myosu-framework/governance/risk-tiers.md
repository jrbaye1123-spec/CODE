# Risk Tiers and Proportionality

Controls are proportionate to tier. Every project, agent capability, and
output pathway is assigned a governance tier.

## Tier 0: Private Provisional Thinking
- Minimal controls. No agent access unless explicitly granted.
- Content: working_idea notes, personal reflections, abandoned threads.
- Retrieval: excluded from agent retrieval by default.
- Audit: not required. Privacy preserved by exclusion.

## Tier 1: Internal Research Support
- Agents may assist with retrieval, extraction, summarization.
- Provenance required on all agent outputs.
- Epistemic markers required.
- No external exposure permitted.
- Review: periodic spot-check sufficient.

## Tier 2: External-Facing Analysis or Collaboration
- Full provenance required on all outputs.
- Human review of all agent synthesis before vault insertion.
- Export audit required.
- Collaborator onboarding required before access.
- Review: quarterly governance review.

## Tier 3: Publication, Third-Party Decision Impact, or Dual-Use
- Maximum controls. Full audit trail required.
- Publication provenance audit mandatory before export.
- Dual-use classification gates enforced.
- Agent synthesis disclosure required in outputs.
- Human reconstruction required for manuscript-critical claims.
- Review: pre-publication audit + quarterly governance review.
