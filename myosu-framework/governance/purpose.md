# Purpose

## Intended Purpose

The Vault is a governed multi-agent research environment built on Obsidian.
Its purpose is to extend John's cognitive capacity through AI-assisted
retrieval, extraction, synthesis, and contradiction detection — while
preserving John's authorship, intellectual provenance, and the right to
think provisionally.

## Intended Users

- **John** — primary user, author, accountable owner.
- **Collaborators** — restricted access, onboarding required.
- **Reviewers** — read-only access to audited outputs.
- **Public readers** — bounded disclosure of AI assistance.

## Affected Parties

- John (author, accountable owner)
- Collaborators (may rely on vault content)
- Cited authors (attribution integrity)
- Research subjects (data minimization, privacy)
- Communities represented in or excluded from sources (retrieval fairness)
- Readers of published outputs (disclosure of AI assistance)

## Problem Being Solved

Research produces an overwhelming volume of sources, claims, and tensions.
Agents help organize and surface patterns. Without governance, agent
assistance erodes authorship, flattens provenance, and creates a knowledge
base where John cannot distinguish his own thinking from agent synthesis.
The constitution preserves the vault as a trustworthy thinking workshop.
