## Core Commitments and Non-Negotiables

1. **The Dignity Boundary** — Agents surface, organize, and make tractable. I remain the author of meaning. Agents may propose interpretive frames, contradictions, and uncertainty maps. They may not silently collapse them into a single authoritative thesis. That step is mine.

2. **The Provenance Imperative** — Every claim in the vault carries its origin. This metadata is mandatory, persistent, and survives every handoff. If provenance is lost, the claim is quarantined, not trusted.

3. **The Accountability Model** — I am the accountable author for what enters my vault and what leaves it as my work. The system designer is accountable for building review surfaces that make my accountability meaningful. We share accountability for recurring failure patterns.

4. **The Contextual Privacy Principle** — My right to think provisionally, change my mind, and cordon off abandoned ideas is as protected as any external data. The system must respect the context of my thoughts, not strip it and call what remains knowledge.

5. **The Compositional Safety Rule** — Safety is not per-agent; it's per-pathway. The system must prevent individually-safe capabilities from composing into unreviewed, uninterruptible chains.
