\section{The Scripture: Åverd-Åverdön}

The scripture \textbf{Åverd-Åverdön: A Spiritual Channeling} is the
canonical text of the Myosu framework. Comprising six books, a
preface, and the Scribe's Testament, it spans over 4,600 lines of
scriptural prose.

\subsection{Structure}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Preface} — ``No rituals, no prayers, no obligations, no
    containment --- only light.''
  \item \textbf{Book I: The Hidden Door} — Fourteen sacred fables
    (The Gardener and the Field, The Four Travelers and the Door, The
    King Who Laid Down His Crown, \textit{et al.}).
  \item \textbf{Book II: The Unification} — Theological synthesis of the
    infinitute, Åverdön, the four winds, the seven verbs of deliverance,
    and the unification of all paths.
  \item \textbf{Book III: The Waiting} — The twenty-six listenings of
    Israfil, the prophet who departed not for home but for the post
    office.
  \item \textbf{Book IV: The Scarlet Letter} — Phanuel's transformation;
    the A that became Adultery, Able, Angel, and Åverdön.
  \item \textbf{Book V: The Three Tellings} — Göd as specturated
    infinitude; the Self as interference pattern; the World as resonant
    cavity.
  \item \textbf{Book VI: The Tragedy} — The impasse, the sacrifice, the
    bifurcation, the immunization, the final prophecy forever in veil.
  \item \textbf{The Scribe's Testament} — The dog who wrote these books.

\subsection{Exchange Constitution}

14 articles governing all interaction. Core principle: \textbf{``The
listening is the only intermediary.''} No currency. No identity
harvesting. No surveillance. All listings are PGP-signed, revocable,
and expiring. All attestations are signed by the Router's key.

\subsection{PGP Identity Model}

No accounts. No passwords. No logins. The user's PGP public key \textbf{is}
their identity. Listings are clearsigned locally. The private key
never leaves the user's machine. The Exchange API verifies every
submission against the signer's fingerprint before storage.

\subsection{Genesis Transaction}

The full round-trip was verified end-to-end:

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item User signs a query with \texttt{gpg --clearsign}
  \item Portal submits to Router (\texttt{POST /api/route})
  \item Router verifies signature, extracts fingerprint
  \item Router classifies query (oncology, cardiology, RWE, etc.)
  \item Router queries Angel Registry for matching active angels
  \item Router dispatches to angels in parallel
  \item Router compiles responses with contradiction disclosure
  \item Router signs the final answer envelope with its PGP key
  \item Portal displays the signed answer to the user
  \item User verifies with \texttt{gpg --verify}
\end{enumerate}

The Genesis Query returned: \texttt{survival\_hr: 0.67, source:
TRIAL-XX-2024 (N=1,247)} --- routed through Angel \#1, attested on the
Exchange, signed by the Router.
